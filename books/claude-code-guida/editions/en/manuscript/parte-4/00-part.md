# Part IV. Control and automation

This part is new in the second edition. In the first one the projects worked, but control over what Claude could do relied mostly on instructions in CLAUDE.md and on your own attention. Today Claude Code starts in auto mode and works for long stretches without asking you anything, so it pays to know exactly where the guardrails are and how to move them.

Five chapters, all on the same small lab project, `cc-advanced-lab`: a Python script, a CSV and a `.env` file that must stay untouched. Chapter 11 covers permission modes, allow, ask and deny rules, and the sandbox. Chapter 12 covers hooks, which turn a "remember to" into a rule that always fires. Chapter 13 covers MCP, to connect Claude to GitHub, a database and the other services you use. Chapter 14 covers plugins, to package all of it and hand it to your team. Chapter 15 covers context and memory, that is, how to keep what Claude sees and remembers under control.

You can also read it after the projects in Parts V and VI. If you work on code that matters, read it first.
