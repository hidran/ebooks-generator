# Part VI — Capstones and Beyond

Three projects, then three chapters about surviving in this ecosystem.

The first two capstones are specified, not solved. They state requirements and acceptance criteria and leave the design to you, because by this point choosing the design *is* the skill. Capstone A is a repository auditor: a plain PHP CLI that reads a codebase, uses tools to explore it, and produces a structured report. Capstone B is an agentic support desk in Laravel, combining retrieval, tools, approval workflows and streaming into something a real support team could use.

Capstone C, in Chapter 26, is the opposite of the other two: a complete application built step by step. An agentic trip planner chooses a destination from real weather data, finds a flight and a hotel, and books them — with a human approving the dates, the offers and the exact amount, and every step surviving pauses and crashes. Each section says what the code does and why, so the chapter doubles as a review of the whole book.

Chapter 27 collects the three things that are never in the documentation. How to choose a version of a fast-moving library and survive it, including how to tell what you actually have installed rather than what a blog post says you have. What the ecosystem around NeuronAI offers, and which parts of it you should depend on. And how to use AI assistance to write this kind of code — which parts it is genuinely good at, and which parts you must keep writing yourself.
